The VCE Chemistry exam (2 h 30 min plus 15 min reading) opens with a multiple-choice section. Allow about 1.5 minutes each. Use your data book. Several of these are deliberately nasty: read every word, and check the units and states.

1. Which fuel releases the most energy per gram when burnt completely?

  1. hydrogen
  2. methane
  3. octane
  4. ethanol

H₂: 141 kJ g⁻¹, far above methane (55.6), octane (47.9) and ethanol (29.6). Per mole it's the lowest of these.

2. In an Ag⁺/Ag ∥ Pb²⁺/Pb galvanic cell, 0.0100 mol of electrons flows. The change in mass of the electrodes is

  1. Ag loses 1.08 g; Pb gains 1.04 g
  2. Ag gains 1.08 g; Pb gains 2.07 g
  3. Ag gains 1.08 g; Pb loses 1.04 g
  4. Ag gains 0.54 g; Pb loses 2.07 g

Ag⁺ (+0.80) is reduced at the cathode: 0.0100 mol Ag deposited = 1.08 g. Pb is oxidised: 0.00500 mol dissolves = 1.04 g lost.

3. Molten potassium bromide is electrolysed with inert electrodes. The products are

  1. H₂ and Br₂
  2. K and Br₂
  3. H₂ and O₂
  4. K and O₂

No water in a melt, so K⁺ is reduced and Br⁻ is oxidised.

4. For \(\ce{2NO(g) + O2(g) <=> 2NO2(g)}\), \(\Delta H < 0\). Which change increases the value of \(K\)?

  1. increasing the pressure
  2. adding a catalyst
  3. adding O₂
  4. decreasing the temperature

Only temperature changes \(K\). For an exothermic forward reaction, lower temperature means larger \(K\). A and C shift the position but leave \(K\) unchanged.

5. A solution calorimeter's temperature falls when a salt dissolves. This means

  1. \(\Delta H\) is positive and energy is absorbed from the water
  2. \(\Delta H\) is negative
  3. the calibration factor is negative
  4. the salt doesn't dissolve completely

Endothermic: the dissolving process takes heat from the solution.

6. How many sets of peaks are in the ¹H NMR spectrum of ethyl ethanoate?

  1. 2
  2. 4
  3. 3
  4. 8

CH₃CO (singlet), OCH₂ (quartet), CH₃ (triplet).

7. The mass spectrum of dichloromethane, CH₂Cl₂, shows a molecular-ion cluster at \(m/z\) = 84, 86 and 88. The approximate ratio of their heights is

  1. 3 : 1 : 0
  2. 9 : 6 : 1
  3. 1 : 2 : 1
  4. 1 : 1 : 1

Each Cl is ³⁵Cl (¾) or ³⁷Cl (¼): \(P(35,35) = \frac{9}{16}\), \(P(\text{one of each}) = 2 \times \frac{3}{16} = \frac{6}{16}\), \(P(37,37) = \frac{1}{16}\). A 1 : 2 : 1 pattern would be two Br atoms.

8. Butan-2-ol is heated with acidified dichromate. The organic product is

  1. butanal
  2. butanoic acid
  3. but-2-ene
  4. butanone

Secondary alcohol → ketone.

9. 20.0 L of ethene burns in 50.0 L of oxygen (\(\ce{C2H4 + 3O2 -> 2CO2 + 2H2O}\)). After cooling to SLC, the gas remaining is

  1. 33.3 L CO₂ and 3.3 L C₂H₄
  2. 40.0 L CO₂ only
  3. 40.0 L CO₂ and 10.0 L O₂
  4. 33.3 L CO₂ only

20 L C₂H₄ needs 60 L O₂; only 50 L is available, so O₂ is limiting. 50 L O₂ burns 16.7 L C₂H₄, making 33.3 L CO₂; 3.3 L C₂H₄ is left. Water is liquid at SLC.

10. In an alkaline hydrogen fuel cell, water is produced

  1. at the cathode only
  2. at both electrodes
  3. at the anode
  4. in the salt bridge

\(\ce{H2 + 2OH- -> 2H2O + 2e-}\) at the anode. (In an acidic PEM cell, water forms at the cathode.)

11. The atom economy for \(\ce{C2H5Br + NaOH -> C2H5OH + NaBr}\) is closest to (\(M\): C₂H₅Br 108.9, NaOH 40.0, C₂H₅OH 46.0)

  1. 100%
  2. 30.9%
  3. 42.2%
  4. 69.1%

\(\frac{46.0}{148.9} \times 100 = 30.9\%\).

12. A student rinses the pipette with deionised water before taking aliquots of an acid to titrate against standard NaOH. The calculated acid concentration will be

  1. unaffected
  2. too high
  3. impossible to determine
  4. too low

The aliquot is diluted, so it contains fewer moles of acid and the titre is smaller. The calculation still assumes the full aliquot volume of undiluted acid, giving a low result.

13. When a protein is denatured by heating,

  1. hydrogen bonds and other interactions in the secondary and tertiary structure are disrupted
  2. peptide bonds are hydrolysed
  3. the amino acid sequence changes
  4. the protein becomes a polysaccharide

The covalent backbone survives; only the folding is lost.

14. Molten NaCl and molten Al₂O₃ (in cryolite) cells are connected in series. When 0.600 mol of Na is produced, the amount of Al produced is

  1. 0.600 mol
  2. 1.80 mol
  3. 0.200 mol
  4. 0.300 mol

Same charge: 0.600 mol e⁻. \(\ce{Al^3+ + 3e- -> Al}\): 0.200 mol.

15. For \(\ce{A(g) <=> 2B(g)}\), \(K = 0.50\ \text{M}\). A 1.0 L flask contains 0.20 mol A and 0.40 mol B. Which is correct?

  1. The system is at equilibrium
  2. \(Q = 0.80\); the amount of A will increase
  3. \(Q = 2.0\); the amount of B will increase
  4. \(Q = 0.80\); the amount of B will increase

\(Q = \frac{0.40^2}{0.20} = 0.80 > K\): net reverse reaction, so A increases.

16. Transesterification of a triglyceride with methanol produces

  1. fatty acids and glycerol only
  2. glucose and fatty acids
  3. ethanol and CO₂
  4. methyl esters (biodiesel) and glycerol

The fatty acid chains swap from glycerol to methanol.

17. In reversed-phase HPLC with a water–methanol mobile phase, which compound would be expected to have the shortest retention time?

  1. ethanoic acid
  2. octane
  3. hexan-1-ol
  4. ethyl hexanoate

The most polar, smallest molecule is least attracted to the non-polar stationary phase, so it elutes first.

18. Which statement about catalysts is correct?

  1. They increase the equilibrium yield
  2. They make ΔH more negative
  3. They increase the rates of the forward and reverse reactions
  4. They are consumed in the reaction

Lower \(E_a\) for both directions; position of equilibrium and ΔH unchanged.

19. The IR spectrum of a compound shows a broad band at 3200–3550 cm⁻¹ and a strong band at 1715 cm⁻¹. Its ¹H NMR spectrum has no peak above 5 ppm. The compound is best described as

  1. a carboxylic acid
  2. a molecule containing both an alcohol OH and a ketone C=O
  3. an ester
  4. an aldehyde

Alcohol-range O–H (not the very broad 2500–3300 acid band) plus C=O. No peak at 9–13 rules out COOH and CHO. E.g. 4-hydroxybutan-2-one.

20. An enzyme from a hot spring bacterium has an optimum temperature of 75 °C. At 37 °C, compared with 75 °C, its activity is expected to be

  1. zero, because it's denatured
  2. higher, because 37 °C is body temperature
  3. the same
  4. lower, but the enzyme isn't denatured

Below the optimum, rate falls because collisions are less frequent and less energetic; the structure is intact.
