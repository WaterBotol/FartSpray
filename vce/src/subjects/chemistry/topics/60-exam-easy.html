\(\frac{8.56\times10^{-3}}{0.01000} = 0.856\ \text{M}\).

Forgetting the dilution (and giving 0.0856 M) was very common. Track the moles backwards through each step.

Express this concentration in g L⁻¹.

  • \(51.4\ \text{g L}^{-1}\)

\(0.856 \times 60.0 = 51.4\ \text{g L}^{-1}\).

Phenolphthalein (colour change pH 8.3–10) is used as the indicator. Explain why it is suitable.

  • Weak acid + strong base: the equivalence point is above pH 7 (basic, because of ethanoate ions)
  • Phenolphthalein changes colour within the steep part of the curve near the equivalence point

A weak acid titrated with a strong base gives an equivalence point above 7, because ethanoate ions are basic. Phenolphthalein changes colour in that range, on the steep part of the curve.

Explain why the burette is rinsed with the NaOH solution rather than with water before filling.

  • Rinsing with water would dilute the NaOH, making the titre too large

Water left in the burette would dilute the NaOH. The titre would be too large and the calculated acid concentration too high.

Amylase is an enzyme in saliva that breaks down starch.

Describe the induced-fit model of enzyme action.

  • The active site is not a rigid, exact fit: it changes shape slightly as the substrate binds
  • This moulds around the substrate, straining its bonds and lowering the activation energy

When the substrate binds, the active site changes shape slightly to fit it more closely. This strains bonds in the substrate and lowers the activation energy.

Explain why amylase activity decreases sharply in the acidic conditions of the stomach.

  • The change in pH changes the charges on R groups (e.g. –COO⁻ → –COOH, –NH₂ → –NH₃⁺)
  • This disrupts ionic interactions and hydrogen bonds in the tertiary structure, changing the active site (denaturation)

Excess H⁺ protonates R groups, changing their charges. The ionic bonds and hydrogen bonds that hold the tertiary structure together are disrupted, the active site changes shape, and the substrate no longer binds.

Vitamin C is water soluble, whereas vitamin D is fat soluble. Explain this difference in terms of structure and bonding.

  • Vitamin C has several –OH groups that form hydrogen bonds with water
  • Vitamin D is mostly a large non-polar hydrocarbon (with one –OH), so it dissolves in non-polar fats through dispersion forces

Vitamin C is small, with many –OH groups that hydrogen-bond with water. Vitamin D is a large, mostly non-polar hydrocarbon with only one –OH, so it interacts better with non-polar fats.

Name the type of link that amylase hydrolyses in starch.

  • Glycosidic link (C–O–C between glucose units)

Glycosidic links between glucose units.

Assessor’s report

General comments

This paper covered all four areas of study at a gentler level than the VCAA exam. Most questions needed one idea or one calculation. Students who knew their standard equations (combustion, galvanic half-equations, esterification, fuel-cell reactions) and could run the \(n = \frac{m}{M}\), \(n = cV\) and \(n = \frac{V}{24.8}\) routines accurately scored well.

Where marks were lost

  • Units and conversions. Minutes left unconverted in \(Q = It\), mL not converted to L in \(n = cV\), and J mixed with kJ in \(\Delta H\) cost more marks than any gap in chemistry knowledge.
  • Signs. \(\Delta H\) for an exothermic reaction must be negative. A calorimetry answer without the sign is not the enthalpy change.
  • Reading the conditions. Alkaline versus acidic fuel cells, and molten versus aqueous electrolytes, change the equations. Circle the electrolyte during reading time.
  • Dilutions in titrations. Question 10c was the most-missed calculation. Work the moles backwards: titre → aliquot → volumetric flask → original sample.
  • Explanations that stop too early. “Powder has a larger surface area” earns 1 mark. Adding “so collisions are more frequent” earns the second.

Advice

If you scored 85% or more, move on to the medium paper. If not, redo the questions you missed using the linked topic pages, then try this paper again under timed conditions in a week.
