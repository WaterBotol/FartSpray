The Chemistry exam

One written exam: 15 minutes reading plus 2 hours 30 minutes writing. Section A is multiple choice; Section B is short and extended answer, including questions on experimental design and data analysis. You're given the VCAA data book (electrochemical series, IR and NMR data, constants, formulas) and can bring an approved scientific calculator. Check the current exam specifications on the VCAA website.
