Definitions worded the way examiners expect. Click the link after each for the full explanation.

A–F

Allele frequency
The proportion of all copies of a gene in a population that are a particular allele. Population genetics
Allopatric speciation
Formation of new species after a geographical barrier stops gene flow between populations. Speciation
Alternative splicing
Different combinations of exons joined from one pre-mRNA, giving different proteins from one gene. Gene expression
Analogous structures
Structures with similar functions but different origins; evidence of convergent evolution. Evidence
Antibody
A Y-shaped protein secreted by plasma cells that binds a specific antigen. Adaptive immunity
Anticodon
Three bases on a tRNA complementary to an mRNA codon. Gene expression
Antigen
A molecule that can be recognised by the immune system and trigger a response. Pathogens
Antigenic drift / shift
Gradual mutation of viral antigens / sudden reassortment of genes between strains. Disease
Attenuation (trp operon)
Early termination of transcription controlled by how fast a ribosome translates the leader sequence. Gene regulation
Bottleneck effect
Genetic drift after a sudden, drastic reduction in population size. Population genetics
C4 / CAM plants
Plants that reduce photorespiration by separating CO₂ fixation in space (C4) or time (CAM). Photosynthesis
cDNA
DNA made from mRNA by reverse transcriptase; lacks introns. Recombinant DNA
Clonal selection
Activation and multiplication of only the lymphocytes whose receptors match an antigen. Adaptive immunity
Codon
A sequence of three mRNA bases specifying an amino acid or stop. Nucleic acids
Coenzyme
A non-protein organic molecule that carries energy, electrons or groups for enzymes (e.g. ATP, NADH). Enzymes
Competitive inhibitor
A molecule that binds the active site, blocking the substrate; overcome by more substrate. Enzymes
CRISPR-Cas9
A bacterial defence system used as a gene-editing tool: a guide RNA directs Cas9 to cut specific DNA. DNA tools
Denaturation
Loss of a protein's 3D shape (and function) when its weak bonds are disrupted. Proteins
Dendritic cell
An antigen-presenting cell that carries antigen to lymph nodes to activate T helper cells. Innate immunity
Exon / intron
Coding sequences kept in mature mRNA / non-coding sequences spliced out. Nucleic acids
Founder effect
Genetic drift in a new population started by a few individuals. Population genetics
Frameshift mutation
An insertion or deletion that changes the reading frame after it. Population genetics

G–P

Gel electrophoresis
Separation of DNA fragments by size in an electric field through a gel. DNA tools
Gene flow
Movement of alleles between populations. Population genetics
Gene pool
All the alleles in a population. Population genetics
Genetic drift
Random change in allele frequencies, strongest in small populations. Population genetics
Herd immunity
Indirect protection of non-immune people when enough of the population is immune. Immunity
Hominin
Humans and extinct relatives after the split from the chimpanzee lineage; habitually bipedal. Human evolution
Homologous structures
Structures with the same underlying anatomy due to common ancestry. Evidence
Hybridoma
A fused B cell–myeloma cell that makes one antibody and divides indefinitely. Disease
MHC class I / II
Self markers on all nucleated cells / on antigen-presenting cells only. Pathogens
Monoclonal antibodies
Identical antibodies specific to one antigen, produced by one clone of cells. Disease
Natural selection
Differential survival and reproduction of individuals due to heritable variation, changing allele frequencies. Population genetics
Non-competitive inhibitor
Binds away from the active site, changing its shape; not overcome by more substrate. Enzymes
Operator
DNA sequence in an operon where a repressor binds. Gene regulation
PCR
Polymerase chain reaction: cycles of denaturation, annealing and extension that amplify DNA. DNA tools
Photorespiration
Rubisco binding O₂ instead of CO₂, reducing photosynthetic efficiency. Photosynthesis
Plasma cell
An activated B cell that secretes large amounts of antibody. Adaptive immunity
Promoter
DNA sequence where RNA polymerase binds to start transcription. Nucleic acids
Proteome
The complete set of proteins expressed by a cell or organism at a given time. Proteins

R–Z

Recombinant plasmid
A plasmid with a foreign gene inserted. Recombinant DNA
Regulatory gene
A gene whose product controls the expression of other genes. Gene regulation
Restriction enzyme (endonuclease)
An enzyme that cuts DNA at a specific recognition sequence. DNA tools
Rubisco
The enzyme that fixes CO₂ onto RuBP in the Calvin cycle. Photosynthesis
Selection pressure
An environmental factor that affects survival and reproduction. Population genetics
Species
A group of organisms that can interbreed in nature to produce viable, fertile offspring. Speciation
Structural gene
A gene coding for a protein with a structural or functional role (not regulating other genes). Gene regulation
Template strand
The DNA strand read by RNA polymerase; complementary to the mRNA. Nucleic acids
Transcription
Making RNA from a DNA template. Gene expression
Transgenic organism
An organism containing a gene from another species. Recombinant DNA
Transitional fossil
A fossil with features intermediate between ancestral and descendant groups. Speciation
Translation
Making a polypeptide from mRNA at a ribosome. Gene expression
Vestigial structure
A reduced structure with little function, functional in ancestors. Evidence
Zoonosis
A disease transmitted from animals to humans. Disease
