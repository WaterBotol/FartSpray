The Biology exam

One written exam: 15 minutes reading plus 2 hours 30 minutes writing. Section A is multiple choice; Section B is short and extended answer, including questions on experimental design, data analysis and unfamiliar scenarios that apply the key knowledge. Check the current exam specifications on the VCAA website.
