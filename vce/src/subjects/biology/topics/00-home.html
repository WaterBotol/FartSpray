Course map

Unit 3 asks how cells maintain life (nucleic acids, proteins, DNA tools, and the pathways of photosynthesis and respiration). Unit 4 asks how life responds to challenges (immunity and disease) and how species change over time.

The Biology exam

One written exam: 15 minutes reading plus 2 hours 30 minutes writing. Section A is multiple choice; Section B is short and extended answer, including questions on experimental design, data analysis and unfamiliar scenarios that apply the key knowledge.
