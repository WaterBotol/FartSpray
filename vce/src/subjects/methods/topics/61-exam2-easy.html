2023 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 1 Open in the exam PDF (p. 2) ↗

Amplitude and period of \(f(x) = -\frac12\sin(3x + 2\pi)\)

Amplitude is always positive: \(\frac12\). Period \(= \frac{2\pi}{3}\).

77% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 2% · B 16% · C 2% · D 3% · E 77%. The most popular wrong answer was B (16%).

2023 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 5 Open in the exam PDF (p. 3) ↗

Which power function has a horizontal tangent at the origin

\(y = x^{4/3}\) has \(y^{\prime} = \frac43 x^{1/3} = 0\) at x = 0. Powers below 1 give vertical tangents or cusps there.

62% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 9% · B 16% · C 8% · D 62% · E 4%. The most popular wrong answer was B (16%).

2023 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 7 Open in the exam PDF (p. 4) ↗

Domain of the derivative of \(\log_e\sqrt{1 - x}\)

The composite exists for x < 1, and its derivative \(-\frac{1}{2(1-x)}\) exists on the same set: \((-\infty, 1)\).

60% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 3% · B 12% · C 60% · D 7% · E 17%. The most popular wrong answer was E (17%).

2023 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 10 Open in the exam PDF (p. 5) ↗

k with Pr(X < k) = 0.35 for a piecewise-linear pdf

\(\frac{(k-1)^2}{40} = 0.35 \Rightarrow k = 1 + \sqrt{14} \approx 4.7\), which lies in the first piece.

60% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 8% · B 60% · C 14% · D 13% · E 5%.

2023 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 15 Open in the exam PDF (p. 7) ↗

Matching two normal pdfs (μ = 100, σ = 20 and μ = 80, σ = 10) to a diagram

The smaller σ gives a taller, narrower curve, and the smaller mean sits further left.

65% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 65% · B 6% · C 17% · D 4% · E 6%. The most popular wrong answer was C (17%).

2023 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 16 Open in the exam PDF (p. 8) ↗

g given \(f(x)g(x) = e^{(x-1)^2}\) with \(f(x) = e^{x-1}\)

\(g = e^{(x-1)^2 - (x-1)} = e^{(x-1)(x-2)}\).

2024 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 1 Open in the exam PDF (p. 2) ↗

Asymptote(s) of \(y = \log_e(x+1) - 3\)

A log graph has only a vertical asymptote: \(x = -1\). The −3 shifts the graph but adds no horizontal asymptote.

74% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 74% · B 2% · C 3% · D 20%. The most popular wrong answer was D (20%).

2024 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 2 Open in the exam PDF (p. 2) ↗

Find \(g(2)\) given \(g^{\prime}(x) = x^3 - x\) and \(g(0) = 5\)

\(g(x) = \frac{x^4}{4} - \frac{x^2}{2} + 5\), so \(g(2) = 4 - 2 + 5 = 7\).

81% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 10% · B 4% · C 5% · D 81%.

2024 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 3 Open in the exam PDF (p. 2) ↗

Conditional probability \(\Pr(X < 4 \mid X > 1)\) from a table in terms of k

13k = 1, then \(\frac{\Pr(X = 2, 3)}{\Pr(X = 2, 3, 4)} = \frac{8k}{10k} = \frac45\).

67% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 7% · B 11% · C 67% · D 14%.

2024 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 4 Open in the exam PDF (p. 3) ↗

\(\int_b^c 2f(x)\,dx\) from two other integrals

\(\int_b^c f = \int_a^c f - \int_a^b f = 3 - (-5) = 8\), and doubling gives 16.

70% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 7% · B 70% · C 12% · D 9%.
