2023 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 2 Open in the exam PDF (p. 2) ↗

Axis of symmetry of \(y = ax^2 + 2bx + c\)

\(x = -\frac{2b}{2a} = -\frac{b}{a}\). The coefficient of x is 2b, not b.

53% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 53% · B 29% · C 3% · D 6% · E 8%. The most popular wrong answer was B (29%).

2023 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 3 Open in the exam PDF (p. 3) ↗

Domain of the sum of two functions with domains [−2, 3) and (−1, 5]

The sum’s domain is the intersection: \((-1, 3)\).

47% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 22% · B 12% · C 11% · D 7% · E 47%. The most popular wrong answer was A (22%).

2023 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 4 Open in the exam PDF (p. 3) ↗

Values of k giving infinitely many solutions

The determinant \(k^2 + k - 20 = 0\) gives k = −5 or 4. Only k = −5 makes the equations identical. k = 4 gives parallel lines.

55% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 15% · B 55% · C 10% · D 14% · E 4%. The most popular wrong answer was A (15%).

\(\int_7^3 f\,dx\) from \(\int_3^{10} f = C\) and \(\int_7^{10} f = D\)

\(\int_3^7 f = C - D\), and reversing the terminals gives \(D - C\).

Range of \(g(f(x))\) with \(f(x) = x^2 - 4x\) on \((1, \infty)\) and \(g(x) = e^{-x}\)

2024 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 6 Open in the exam PDF (p. 3) ↗

Inverse of \(f(x) = \frac{2x+1}{3-x}\) and its domain

Swap x and y and solve: \(y = 3 - \frac{7}{x+2}\). The domain of \(f^{-1}\) is the range of f, which is \(R \setminus \{-2\}\).

50% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 31% · B 50% · C 7% · D 12%. The most popular wrong answer was A (31%).

2024 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 14 Open in the exam PDF (p. 7) ↗

\(\Pr(X < 0.5)\) for a piecewise-linear pdf with unknown k

The total area \(4k - 1 = 1\) gives \(k = \frac12\). Then \(\Pr(X < 0.5) = 1 - \frac{1}{16} = \frac{15}{16}\).

53% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 9% · B 53% · C 30% · D 7%. The most popular wrong answer was C (30%).

Points of inflection of \(y = 2 - \tan\left(\pi\left(x - \frac14\right)\right)\)

\(\tan\) inflects where its argument is \(k\pi\): \(x = k + \frac14\), \(y = 2\).

53% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 53% · B 19% · C 13% · D 15%. The most popular wrong answer was B (19%).

2024 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 18 Open in the exam PDF (p. 10) ↗

Value of x maximising the area of a trapezium with slant sides 10

\(A = 2x\sqrt{100 - x^2}\). Maximise \(x^2(100 - x^2)\): \(x^2 = 50\), so \(x = 5\sqrt2\).

58% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 18% · B 58% · C 12% · D 10%. The most popular wrong answer was A (18%).

2025 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 4 Open in the exam PDF (p. 4) ↗

Values of k for which a 2×2 linear system has no solution

The determinant \(2k^2 + k - 6 = 0\) gives \(k = -2\) or \(\frac32\). \(k = \frac32\) gives the same line twice (infinitely many solutions), so only \(k = -2\).

55% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 55% · B 13% · C 19% · D 12%. The most popular wrong answer was C (19%).

The trapezium rule overestimates when the curve is concave up on the interval. \(x^3 + 1\) is concave up on [0, 1].

Solving \((f \circ g)(x) > 0\) for \(f(x) = 2x^2 + x - 1\), \(g(x) = \sin x\)

\((2\sin x - 1)(\sin x + 1) > 0\). Since \(\sin x \ge -1\), you need \(\frac12 < \sin x \le 1\).
