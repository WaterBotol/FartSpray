2023 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 9 Open in the exam PDF (p. 5) ↗

Value of a that makes a tan/sin hybrid continuous and smooth at x = 2π

Continuity needs \(\sin(2\pi a) = 0\). Smoothness needs \(a\cos(2\pi a) = \frac12\) (the gradient of \(\tan\frac x2\) there). So \(a = -\frac12\).
