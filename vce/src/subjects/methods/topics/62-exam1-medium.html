2023 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 9 Open in the exam PDF (p. 12) ↗

Two walking tracks (a cubic and a quadratic): find the parameters, a shared turning point, and the maximum area of a triangle

State average for the whole question: 2.7 / 6 (45%).

Verify \(a = 12\) and \(b = -3\)

  • \(f(0) = a = 12\) and \(g(1) = 12 + b = 9 \Rightarrow b = -3\)

Direct substitution.

State average 0.9 / 1 (90%); 90% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 10% · 1: 90%

Verify both have a turning point at P and give P

  • \(f^{\prime}(x) = -(x-2)(3x-2)\) and \(g^{\prime}(x) = 12 - 6x\), both zero at x = 2
  • \(P = (2, 12)\)

Both derivatives vanish at x = 2, and \(f(2) = g(2) = 12\).

State average 1 / 2 (50%); 30% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 34% · 1: 36% · 2: 30%

Maximum area of triangle OAB with A(k, 0), B(k, g(k))

  • \(A(k) = \frac12 k(12k - 3k^2) = 6k^2 - \frac32 k^3\)
  • \(A^{\prime}(k) = 12k - \frac92 k^2 = 0 \Rightarrow k = \frac83\)
  • Maximum area \(\frac{128}{9}\) km²

\(k = \frac83\) gives \(\frac{128}{9}\ \text{km}^2\).

State average 0.8 / 3 (27%); 13% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 65% · 1: 5% · 2: 17% · 3: 13%

2024 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 2 Open in the exam PDF (p. 3) ↗

Value of k for which a pair of simultaneous linear equations has no solution

State average for the whole question: 1.7 / 3 (57%).

Find k for no solution

  • Sets the determinant (or gradient ratio) to zero: \(3k^2 + 2k - 8 = 0\)
  • \(k = \frac43\) or \(k = -2\)
  • Checks: \(k = -2\) gives identical lines (infinitely many), so \(k = \frac43\)

\(k = \frac43\). You must reject \(k = -2\), which gives the same line twice.

State average 1.7 / 3 (57%); 37% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 22% · 1: 22% · 2: 18% · 3: 37%

Sketch the truncus \(g(x) = \frac{1}{(x+3)^2} - 2\); area between it and the x-axis from x = −2 to 0

State average for the whole question: 2.9 / 5 (58%).

Sketch with asymptotes and intercepts

  • Asymptotes \(x = -3\) and \(y = -2\)
  • y-intercept \(\left(0, -\frac{17}{9}\right)\)
  • x-intercepts \(\left(-3 \pm \frac{\sqrt2}{2}, 0\right)\), symmetric about x = −3

Truncus above \(y = -2\), symmetric about \(x = -3\), crossing the x-axis at \(-3 \pm \frac{1}{\sqrt2}\).

State average 2.1 / 3 (70%); 42% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 8% · 1: 16% · 2: 35% · 3: 42%

Area bounded by x = −2, the axes and the graph

  • Sets up \(-\int_{-2}^{0}\left(\frac{1}{(x+3)^2} - 2\right)dx\) (region below the axis)
  • Area \(= \frac{10}{3}\)

\(\int_{-2}^0 g\,dx = \frac23 - 4 = -\frac{10}{3}\), so the area is \(\frac{10}{3}\).

State average 0.8 / 2 (40%); 27% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 48% · 1: 25% · 2: 27%

2024 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 4 Open in the exam PDF (p. 5) ↗

\(X \sim \text{Bi}\left(4, \frac{9}{10}\right)\): standard deviation and \(\Pr(X < 2)\)

State average for the whole question: 1.6 / 3 (53%).

Standard deviation

  • \(\sqrt{4 \times 0.9 \times 0.1} = 0.6\)

\(0.6\). Many gave the variance (0.36) instead.

State average 0.6 / 1 (60%); 59% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 41% · 1: 59%

\(\Pr(X < 2)\)

  • \(\Pr(X=0) + \Pr(X=1) = 0.1^4 + 4(0.9)(0.1)^3\)
  • \(= 0.0037\)

\(0.0001 + 0.0036 = 0.0037\). Watch the decimal places.

State average 1 / 2 (50%); 39% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 38% · 1: 23% · 2: 39%

2024 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 5 Open in the exam PDF (p. 6) ↗

Population model \(h(t) = \frac{3000}{t+1}\): transformations to halve the rate; a confidence interval and sample size

State average for the whole question: 3.1 / 6 (52%).

Population after four years

  • \(600\)

\(h(4) = 600\)

State average 1 / 1 (100%); 95% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 5% · 1: 95%

Two transformations taking \(h\) to \(h_1\) with \(h_1(0) = h(0)\) and half the rate of decrease

  • Dilation by a factor of \(\frac12\) from the t-axis
  • Then a translation of 1500 up (or up 3000 then dilate by \(\frac12\))

\(h_1(t) = \frac12 h(t) + 1500\). Most students tried a horizontal dilation, which changes the timing, not the rate at each time.

State average 0.4 / 2 (20%); 2% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 69% · 1: 29% · 2: 2%

Approximate 95% CI with \(\hat p = 0.6\), n = 100, z = 2

  • \(\text{sd}(\hat P) = \sqrt{\frac{0.6 \times 0.4}{100}} = \frac{\sqrt6}{50}\)
  • CI \(= \left(0.6 - \frac{\sqrt6}{25},\ 0.6 + \frac{\sqrt6}{25}\right)\)

\(0.6 \pm 2 \times \frac{\sqrt6}{50}\)

State average 1.3 / 2 (65%); 44% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 19% · 1: 37% · 2: 44%

Smallest n giving a standard deviation of \(\frac{\sqrt2}{50}\)

  • \(n = 300\)

\(\frac{0.24}{n} = \frac{2}{2500} \Rightarrow n = 300\)

State average 0.4 / 1 (40%); 33% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 67% · 1: 33%

2025 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 2 Open in the exam PDF (p. 3) ↗

Find \(g(x)\) given \(g^{\prime}(x) = \frac{1}{2x+3}\) and \(g(1) = 0\)

State average for the whole question: 1.2 / 2 (60%).

Antidifferentiate and use the condition

  • \(g(x) = \tfrac12\log_e(2x+3) + c\)
  • \(c = -\tfrac12\log_e 5\), so \(g(x) = \tfrac12\log_e\!\left(\frac{2x+3}{5}\right)\)

\(g(x) = \tfrac12\log_e(2x+3) - \tfrac12\log_e 5\). Don’t forget the \(\tfrac12\) from the chain rule.

State average 1.2 / 2 (60%); 40% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 22% · 1: 38% · 2: 40%
