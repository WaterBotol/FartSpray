Exam 2 is 2 hours (plus 15 min reading): Section A is 20 multiple-choice questions (1 mark each); Section B is extended response (about 60 marks). Budget about 1.5 minutes per MCQ and 1.5 minutes per mark in Section B. Use CAS for heavy algebra, but always write the setup (what you define, what you solve) in Section B: that's where the method marks are.

Section A: multiple choice

1. The range of \(f: (-1, 2] \to \mathbb{R},\ f(x) = x^2 - 2x - 3\) is

  1. \([-4, 0]\)
  2. \((-4, 0)\)
  3. \([-4, 0)\)
  4. \([-3, 0)\)
  5. \((-4, -3]\)

\(f(x) = (x - 1)^2 - 4\): minimum \(-4\) at \(x = 1\) (included). \(f(-1) = 0\) but \(-1\) is excluded, and nothing else gives 0 (\(f(2) = -3\)). So \([-4, 0)\).

2. The average rate of change of \(f(x) = \log_e(2x + 1)\) over \([0, 4]\) is closest to

  1. 0.222
  2. 0.549
  3. 0.405
  4. 2.197
  5. 0.111

\(\frac{\log_e 9 - \log_e 1}{4} = \frac{\log_e 3}{2} \approx 0.549\). D is \(\log_e 9\) (forgot to divide by 4). The instantaneous rate at \(x = 4\) is \(\frac{2}{9}\) (option A).

3. The equation \(|x^2 - 4| = k\) has exactly two solutions when

  1. \(0 < k < 4\)
  2. \(k = 4\)
  3. \(k > 4\) only
  4. \(k \ge 4\)
  5. \(k = 0\) or \(k > 4\)

Slide \(y = k\) over the W shape: \(k = 0\) touches at \(\pm2\) (2 solutions); \(0 < k < 4\) gives 4; \(k = 4\) gives 3 (including the peak at \(x = 0\)); \(k > 4\) gives 2.

4. The largest interval containing \(x = 0\) on which \(f(x) = \sin\left(2x - \frac{\pi}{3}\right)\) is one-to-one is

  1. \(\left[-\frac{\pi}{12}, \frac{5\pi}{12}\right]\)
  2. \(\left[-\frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{\pi}{4}\right]\)
  3. \(\left[0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]\)
  4. \(\left[-\frac{\pi}{6}, \frac{\pi}{3}\right]\)
  5. \(\left[-\frac{7\pi}{12}, -\frac{\pi}{12}\right]\)

\(\sin\) is increasing when \(2x - \frac{\pi}{3} \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]\), i.e. \(x \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{12}, \frac{5\pi}{12}\right]\), which contains 0.

5. \(X \sim \text{Bi}(n, 0.4)\). The smallest \(n\) for which \(P(X = 0) < 0.01\) is

  1. 5
  2. 8
  3. 9
  4. 10
  5. 11

\(0.6^n < 0.01 \Rightarrow n > 9.015\), so \(n = 10\).

6. \(X\) is normally distributed with \(P(X < 10) = 0.3\) and \(P(X > 20) = 0.1\). The mean of \(X\) is closest to

  1. 15.0
  2. 12.9
  3. 13.7
  4. 11.4
  5. 14.3

\(\frac{10 - \mu}{\sigma} = -0.5244\), \(\frac{20 - \mu}{\sigma} = 1.2816\). Solving: \(\sigma \approx 5.537\), \(\mu \approx 12.90\).

7. A random variable has pdf \(f(x) = 3x^2\) for \(0 \le x \le 1\). Its variance is

  1. \(\frac34\)
  2. \(\frac35\)
  3. \(\frac{3}{80}\)
  4. \(\frac{9}{16}\)
  5. \(\frac{3}{20}\)

\(E(X) = \frac34\), \(E(X^2) = \frac35\). \(\operatorname{Var} = \frac35 - \frac{9}{16} = \frac{3}{80}\).

8. The number of tangents to \(y = x^3\) that pass through the point \((1, 0)\) is

  1. 0
  2. 1
  3. 2
  4. 3
  5. infinitely many

Tangent at \(x = a\): \(y = 3a^2x - 2a^3\). Through \((1, 0)\): \(3a^2 - 2a^3 = 0 \Rightarrow a = 0\) or \(a = \frac32\). Two tangents (\(y = 0\) and \(y = \frac{27}{4}x - \frac{27}{4}\)).

9. The system \(kx + 2y = 4,\ 8x + ky = k\) has no solution when

  1. \(k = 4\) only
  2. \(k = -4\) only
  3. \(k = 0\)
  4. \(k \in \mathbb{R}\setminus\{-4, 4\}\)
  5. \(k \in \{-4, 4\}\)

Determinant \(k^2 - 16 = 0\). \(k = 4\): \(2x + y = 2\) vs \(2x + y = 1\) (parallel). \(k = -4\): \(-2x + y = 2\) vs \(-2x + y = 1\) (parallel). Both give no solution; never assume one of them is the “infinitely many” case.

10. A transformation that maps \(y = \log_e x\) onto \(y = \log_e(2x - 4) + 1\) is

  1. \((x, y) \to \left(\frac{x}{2} + 2,\ y + 1\right)\)
  2. \((x, y) \to (2x + 4,\ y + 1)\)
  3. \((x, y) \to \left(\frac{x}{2} + 4,\ y + 1\right)\)
  4. \((x, y) \to (2x - 4,\ y + 1)\)
  5. \((x, y) \to \left(\frac{x + 4}{2},\ y - 1\right)\)

\(\log_e(2(x - 2)) + 1\): dilate by \(\frac12\) from the \(y\)-axis, translate 2 right and 1 up.

11. An approximate 95% confidence interval for \(p\) is \((0.1, 0.3)\). The sample size is closest to

  1. 31
  2. 61
  3. 16
  4. 100
  5. 246

\(\hat p = 0.2\), \(M = 0.1\): \(n = \frac{1.96^2 \times 0.16}{0.01} \approx 61.5\), so about 61.

12. The trapezium rule with 4 equal strips is used to estimate \(\int_0^4\sqrt{x}\,dx\). The estimate and its relationship to the exact value are

  1. \(2 + \sqrt2 + \sqrt3\), an overestimate
  2. \(1 + \sqrt2 + \sqrt3\), an underestimate
  3. \(\frac{16}{3}\), exact
  4. \(2 + \sqrt2 + \sqrt3\), an underestimate
  5. \(4 + 2\sqrt2 + 2\sqrt3\), an overestimate

\(\frac12\big(0 + 2(1 + \sqrt2 + \sqrt3) + 2\big) = 2 + \sqrt2 + \sqrt3 \approx 5.146 < \frac{16}{3}\). \(\sqrt x\) is concave down, so trapeziums underestimate.

Section B: extended response

Question 1 (functions and calculus). Let \(f(x) = x^3 - 6x^2 + 9x\).

a. Find the coordinates and nature of the stationary points. (3 marks)

b. Find the equation of the tangent at the origin, and the other point where it meets the graph. (3 marks)

c. Find the area enclosed between the graph and this tangent. (2 marks)

d. The graph is translated \(k\) units vertically. Find the values of \(k\) for which the image has three \(x\)-intercepts. (2 marks)

a. \(f'(x) = 3x^2 - 12x + 9 = 3(x - 1)(x - 3) = 0\). [1] Local max \((1, 4)\), local min \((3, 0)\). [2]

b. \(f'(0) = 9\), tangent \(y = 9x\). [1] \(x^3 - 6x^2 + 9x = 9x \Rightarrow x^2(x - 6) = 0\). [1] Other point \((6, 54)\). [1]

c. \(\int_0^6\big(9x - f(x)\big)\,dx = \int_0^6(6x^2 - x^3)\,dx = 432 - 324 = 108\) square units. [2]

d. The image is \(y = f(x) + k\): max at \(4 + k\), min at \(k\). Need \(4 + k > 0\) and \(k < 0\): \(-4 < k < 0\). [2]

Question 2 (probability and statistics). Bolt lengths are \(L \sim \text{N}(50, 0.4^2)\) mm. Bolts shorter than 49.3 mm or longer than 50.7 mm are rejected.

a. Find the proportion rejected, to 4 decimal places. (2 marks)

b. Bolts are packed in boxes of 20. Find the probability that a box contains at most one rejected bolt. (2 marks)

c. Given a bolt is accepted, find the probability its length exceeds 50.5 mm. (2 marks)

d. In a random sample of 400 bolts from a new machine, 36 were rejected. Find an approximate 95% confidence interval for the rejection rate, and comment on whether the new machine appears different from the old one. (3 marks)

e. The old machine is recalibrated so that only 1% of bolts are rejected, with the mean still 50 mm. Find the new standard deviation. (2 marks)

a. \(P(L < 49.3) + P(L > 50.7) = 2P(Z > 1.75) \approx 0.0801\). [2]

b. \(R \sim \text{Bi}(20, 0.0801)\): \(P(R \le 1) \approx 0.5161\). [2]

c. \(\frac{P(50.5 < L < 50.7)}{P(49.3 \le L \le 50.7)} = \frac{0.0656}{0.9199} \approx 0.0713\). [2]

d. \(\hat p = 0.09\); SE \(\approx 0.01431\); CI \(\approx (0.0620, 0.1180)\). [2] The old rate 0.0801 is inside the interval, so there's no clear evidence the new machine is different. [1]

e. \(P(|L - 50| > 0.7) = 0.01 \Rightarrow \frac{0.7}{\sigma} = z_{0.995} = 2.5758\), so \(\sigma \approx 0.272\) mm. [2]

Question 3 (circular functions model). The depth of water at a jetty is \(D(t) = 5 + 3\sin\left(\frac{\pi t}{6}\right)\) m, \(t\) hours after midnight.

a. State the period and the maximum and minimum depths. (2 marks)

b. Find all times in the first 24 hours when the depth is 6.5 m. (2 marks)

c. A boat needs at least 3.5 m of water. For how many hours per day can it not use the jetty? (3 marks)

d. Find the rate at which the depth is changing at 2 am. (1 mark)

e. Find the average depth between midnight and 3 am, exactly. (2 marks)

a. Period 12 h; max 8 m; min 2 m. [2]

b. \(\sin\frac{\pi t}{6} = \frac12\): \(t = 1, 5, 13, 17\) (1 am, 5 am, 1 pm, 5 pm). [2]

c. \(D < 3.5 \iff \sin\frac{\pi t}{6} < -\frac12\). [1] In each 12-hour cycle: \(\frac{\pi t}{6} \in \left(\frac{7\pi}{6}, \frac{11\pi}{6}\right) \Rightarrow t \in (7, 11)\): 4 hours. [1] Two cycles per day: 8 hours. [1]

d. \(D'(t) = \frac{\pi}{2}\cos\frac{\pi t}{6}\); \(D'(2) = \frac{\pi}{2} \cdot \frac12 = \frac{\pi}{4} \approx 0.785\) m/h. [1]

e. \(\frac13\int_0^3 D\,dt = \frac13\left(15 + \frac{18}{\pi}\right) = 5 + \frac{6}{\pi}\) m (≈ 6.91 m). [2]
