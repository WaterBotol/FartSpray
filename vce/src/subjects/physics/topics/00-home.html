The Physics exam

The end-of-year written exam has 15 minutes of reading time and 2 hours 30 minutes of writing time. Section A is multiple choice. Section B is short and extended answer, including questions on practical work and data analysis. You get a formula sheet and data sheet and may bring an approved scientific calculator. See the Physics exam technique page and the formula sheet.

Numerical answers in this guide use the VCAA data-sheet values (\(g = 9.8\ \text{m s}^{-2}\), \(h = 6.63\times10^{-34}\ \text{J s}\) and so on).
