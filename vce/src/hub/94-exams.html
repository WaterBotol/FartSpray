Every paper here is made of real VCAA exam questions. Every question from the recent exams was ranked by how Victorian students actually scored on it: percentage correct for multiple choice, and state average ÷ marks for written questions. The easiest band became the easier paper, the middle band the medium paper, and the hardest band the harder paper.

  • Easier: questions most of the state got right. Dropping marks here costs the most on exam day.
  • Medium: the middle band, where most study scores are decided.
  • Harder: the questions that separated the top students. Beating the state average here is excellent.

Each question links to the official VCAA exam PDF at the right page (the question text is VCAA copyright, so it isn’t copied here). The assessor’s report after each paper uses the real published results: the state average on those exact questions, the most popular wrong answers, and the parts that cost students the most marks. Physics and Chemistry include some 2022–23 questions from the previous study design, and English uses whole sections, because each section is built around one text. Each paper flags these.
