The gauntlet pulls multiple-choice questions at random from every topic page you select. Trick questions are written to catch a specific misconception, so read every word. Each answer is saved to your progress, and wrong answers go straight to your review list.

  • Start with one subject and one area of study on “Trick questions only”. When you score 8/10 or better, add another area.
  • After each wrong answer, open the source topic (the link under each question) and re-read the section it came from.
  • In the final weeks, run 40-question “Everything” sets for a whole subject under time pressure (about 1 minute per question).
