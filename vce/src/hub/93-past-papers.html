These link straight to the official PDFs and reports on the VCAA website (they open there; nothing is copied here). Work through them roughly in this order: our graded practice exams first, then the current-design VCAA papers under exam conditions, then the previous design for extra volume.

  • Time it. Reading time, then writing time, no notes (except the official formula sheet or data book).
  • Mark it with the examiner's report, not just the answers. The report says what earned each mark and what didn't.
  • Log every lost mark by cause: didn't know it, misread it, or couldn't express it. Then fix the cause, not just the question.
  • NHT papers (Northern Hemisphere Timetable) are sat mid-year by students on a northern-hemisphere school year, and they follow the previous year's study design. A 2025 NHT paper tests the 2024 design.
