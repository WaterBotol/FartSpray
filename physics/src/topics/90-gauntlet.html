The gauntlet pulls multiple-choice questions at random from every topic page. Trick questions are written to catch a specific misconception, so read every word. Each answer is saved to your progress; wrong answers go to your review list automatically.

  • Start with one area of study on “Trick questions only”. When you score 8/10 or better, add another area.
  • After each wrong answer, open the source topic (link under each question) and read the section it came from.
  • In the final weeks, run 40-question “Everything” sets under time pressure (about 1 minute per question).
