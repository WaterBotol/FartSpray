Unit 3 AoS 1: Motion

Constant acceleration

\[ v = u + at \qquad s = ut + \tfrac12 at^2 \]\[ v^2 = u^2 + 2as \qquad s = \tfrac12(u + v)t \]

Straight-line motion with constant \(a\). Projectiles: use vertically with \(a = g\). Projectiles

Newton's second law

\[ F_{\text{net}} = ma \]

Net (vector sum) force. Newton's laws

Weight

\[ F_g = mg \]

Apparent weight = normal force: \(N = m(g + a)\) in a lift (up positive).

Uniform circular motion

\[ v = \frac{2\pi r}{T} \qquad a = \frac{v^2}{r} = \frac{4\pi^2 r}{T^2} \qquad F_{\text{net}} = \frac{mv^2}{r} \]

Net force towards the centre. Circular motion

Banked track (design speed)

\[ \tan\theta = \frac{v^2}{rg} \qquad N = \frac{mg}{\cos\theta} \]

No friction needed at the design speed.

Vertical circles (top and bottom)

\[ \text{bottom: } N - mg = \frac{mv^2}{r} \qquad \text{top: } N + mg = \frac{mv^2}{r} \]\[ v_{\min,\text{top}} = \sqrt{gr} \]

Rollercoasters

Momentum and impulse

\[ p = mv \qquad F\Delta t = m\Delta v = \Delta p \]\[ m_1u_1 + m_2u_2 = m_1v_1 + m_2v_2 \]

Momentum

Work and energy

\[ W = Fs \qquad E_k = \tfrac12 mv^2 \qquad \Delta E_g = mg\Delta h \]

Work = area under F–s graph. Work & energy

Springs

\[ F = -kx \qquad E_s = \tfrac12 kx^2 \]

\(E_s\) = area under F–x graph. Springs

Unit 3 AoS 2: Fields

Gravitation

\[ F_g = G\frac{m_1m_2}{r^2} \qquad g = \frac{GM}{r^2} \]

\(r\) from the centre. Gravity

Satellites

\[ \frac{GMm}{r^2} = \frac{mv^2}{r} = \frac{4\pi^2mr}{T^2} \]\[ v = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{r}} \qquad T^2 = \frac{4\pi^2}{GM}r^3 \]

Satellites

Point charges

\[ F = k\frac{q_1q_2}{r^2} \qquad E = k\frac{q}{r^2} \]

Electric fields

Uniform electric field

\[ E = \frac{V}{d} \qquad F = qE \qquad W = qV \]

\(\tfrac12 mv^2 = qV\) from rest.

Force on a current

\[ F = nIlB \quad (I \perp B) \]

Zero when \(I \parallel B\). Motors

Charge in a magnetic field

\[ F = qvB \qquad r = \frac{mv}{qB} \]

From \(qvB = mv^2/r\). Particles
